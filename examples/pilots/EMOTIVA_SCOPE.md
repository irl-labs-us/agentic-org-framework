# Emotiva organization assessment — proposed scope

Status: **scoping hypothesis; owner confirmation pending**. Prepared November 14, 2026 from existing local materials only. No new external access, participant interview, workflow observation or production test was performed for this scope.

The user selected Emotiva as the organization pilot. The specific decision, participating owners, internal evidence access and product portfolio remain to be established. This document proposes a bounded starting point rather than silently deciding those matters.

## Candidate decision

**Hypothesis for owner confirmation:** Which smallest change to the founder journey across Emotiva's website, PROPEL and human consulting handoffs should receive a bounded trial, and what evidence would justify continuing it?

This candidate follows the historical revised consulting brief's focus on one useful founder task and a small trial. It is not a claim that this is Emotiva's current priority. The owner may instead choose an internal workflow, portfolio allocation or evidence-readiness decision. Until confirmed, do not fund implementation or represent participants as committed.

Proposed scope: the owner-selected relationship between website entry, PROPEL task completion and a human consulting handoff. These are candidate surfaces, not a verified inventory of current products. Preserve each product's canonical strategy; identify organizational constraints and an accountable cross-product decision owner separately.

Select **two or three** consequential workflows from these hypotheses:

1. Founder identifies the right offering and reaches an appropriate next step.
2. Founder completes one useful task and understands, reviews and corrects its output.
3. Participant hands work/evidence to a human consultant and receives a decision or next action.

Owner confirmation must name the actual portfolio, workflow boundaries, consequential actions, exclusions, lifecycle/exposure, budget and stopping point. Do not expand to the entire company by implication.

## Options to compare

| Option | Assumption to test | Evidence needed before recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Clarify a manual process or handoff | Ambiguous roles/instructions account for a material part of friction | Observed handoff cases, participant explanation and before/after comparison |
| Reuse an existing tool | A current tool can support the task with acceptable effort | Actual capability, permissions, integration and ownership evidence |
| Conventional automation | The task can be specified reliably with rules | Representative inputs/exceptions, failure recovery and operating cost |
| AI-assisted task | Ambiguity warrants model behavior and review burden is acceptable | Authorized data, representative evals, correction costs and boundary tests |
| Defer change; acquire evidence | Decision-critical uncertainty dominates expected value | Named missing evidence, bounded collection plan and decision date |

Compare setup, operation, review, correction, data preparation, evaluation, maintenance and reversal costs. Use measured baselines before targets; no numerical ROI or staffing judgment is asserted here. Affected-domain validation is required before accepting a diagnosis.
