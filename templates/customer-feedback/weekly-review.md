## Executive customer outcome

State whether customers were safer, more able to complete their goals, and more
in control than the prior week. Do not substitute ticket counts for an outcome.

## Material postmortems

For each material event record:

- customer expectation and consequence;
- trigger and contributing system conditions;
- why existing tests/review/detection did not prevent it;
- customer recovery and rescue burden;
- immediate containment;
- systemic prevention and affected happy paths;
- evidence and uncertainty, without private content.

Explicitly list non-priorities so deferral is visible. Security/functionality
blockers come first; among passing solutions, customer experience outranks cost
and convenience.

## Synthetic and journey updates

Record persona attributes, boundary fixtures, recovery cases, and browser paths
to add or revise. State the abstracted pattern and confirm no customer content was
copied.

## Questions and evidence gaps

List conflicts in intended happy paths, missing provenance, and decisions that
require `{CEO}`. Preserve the safe default while awaiting an answer.
